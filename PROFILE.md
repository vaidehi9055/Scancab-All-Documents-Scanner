## 🌟 Featured Project

### 📄 [ScanCab: All Documents Scanner](https://github.com/vaidehi9055/Scancab-All-Documents-Scanner)

An Android app that turns your phone into a smart pocket scanner.

- 🎯 Auto edge detection and one-tap cropping
- 🔤 OCR to extract, edit, and copy text
- 🔳 QR scanner and generator, plus a barcode scanner
- 🪪 ID card scanner (single or two side, print-ready)
- ✏️ Built-in editor: filters, adjust, highlight, text, overlays, and effects
- 📄 High-quality PDF export and instant sharing

**Role:** Android Developer & UI/UX Designer (Lead)
**Team:** Bhavin Mulani · Parth Kothiya · Sagar Sorathiya

➡️ [Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scan.scancab&pcampaignid=web_share)
