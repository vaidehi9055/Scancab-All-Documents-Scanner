# 📄 ScanCab - PDF Document Scanner

ScanCab is a powerful and simple PDF document scanner app that turns your phone into a portable scanner. Easily scan documents, convert them into PDFs, and share them instantly.

ScanCab allows you to scan receipts, notes, bills, business cards, and documents in seconds — anytime, anywhere.

---

# 📱 App Overview

ScanCab helps users:

- Scan documents quickly
- Convert documents into PDF
- Share scanned files instantly
- Store digital copies
- Organize important documents

No need for a physical scanner.  
ScanCab makes document scanning simple and fast.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 📄 PDF Document Scanner
Scan documents and convert them into high-quality PDF files.

## 📷 Camera Scanner
Use your phone camera to scan documents easily.

## ⚡ Fast Document Scanning
Scan documents quickly within seconds.

## 📤 Easy Sharing
Share scanned PDFs with friends, clients, or co-workers.

## 📱 Portable Scanner
Turn your phone into a pocket document scanner.

## 🧾 Scan Notes & Receipts
Scan receipts, invoices, notes, and business documents.

## 🎯 Simple & Clean UI
Easy-to-use interface for smooth user experience.
